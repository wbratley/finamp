# Finamp (Fork)

This is a personal fork of [Finamp](https://github.com/jmshrv/finamp) — a free, open-source Jellyfin music player for Android and iOS that lets you stream and download your media library with a modern, Spotify-like interface.

## What this fork adds

- **Video library browsing** — browse and play content from video libraries, organized by channel with support for nested folder structures
- **Continue tab** — a dedicated tab showing in-progress videos with resumable playback from where you left off
- **Progress tracking** — progress bars and played badges on video items so you always know what you've watched
- **10-second seek buttons** — quick forward/back seek controls during video playback
- **Android Auto media browsing** — full media browsing support through Android Auto
- **Audio from video libraries** — play audio from home video and music video libraries
- **Premiere date display** — shows premiere dates in video list subtitles

## Getting this fork

Download the latest APK from the [releases page](https://github.com/wbratley/finamp/releases).

This fork is intended for sideloading via [Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium) or direct APK install. It is not published to any app store.

## Original project

For the full Finamp experience including App Store / Google Play / F-Droid releases, translations, and community support, visit the [original Finamp repository](https://github.com/jmshrv/finamp).
